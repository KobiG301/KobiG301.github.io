Movie Theater

This program calculates the total revenue from movie ticket sales based on customer ages. The system asks the user to enter the ages of eight people. For each age entered, the program determines the ticket price using the following rules: children aged 12 or younger pay $8, people between the ages of 13 and 64 pay $12, and seniors aged 65 or older pay $10. Each ticket price is added to a running total, and after all eight ages have been entered, the program outputs the total revenue from all ticket sales.
